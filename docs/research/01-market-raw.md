> Method caveat: the container's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches on most Tunisian domains; figures come from search-engine summaries of the cited pages. **Spot-check key numbers before using them externally.**

## 1. Macro — public recruitment 2026
- **LF 2026**: 51,878 recruitments + régularisations; art. 9 caps authorised headcount at 687,000; ~23,000 net new hires. [lapresse](https://lapresse.tn/2025/10/16/fonction-publique-letat-tunisien-relance-les-recrutements-en-2026/) · [webmanagercenter](https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/11/03/554950/recrutement-dans-la-fonction-publique-les-departements-qui-recruteront-le-plus-et-le-moins-en-2026/)
- By ministry: Education ~18,079 (ministry budget: 18,349), Health ~4,000, Defence 3,728, Interior 2,695 (minister: 2,900 = 1,000 Sûreté + 1,500 Garde nationale + 400 Protection civile), Finance 1,754, Justice 1,196. [webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-securite-nationale-recrutements-et-achats-dhelicopteres-pour-2026/386885/) · [allafrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202510300467.html)
- Categories: 12,942 ouvriers de chantiers regularised, 1,350 PhD posts, 1,066 PE teachers, 2,601 licence appliquée holders. [allafrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202510240500.html)
- Our computation: ≥ 26,779 of the 51,878 are régularisations of people already in post ⇒ **exam-based volume is much smaller than the headline.**
- 2027: budget circular n°2 of 14 Apr 2026 restricts hiring to priority sectors. [allafrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202604200506.html)
- **Loi n°2025-18 (22 Dec 2025)** — exceptional recruitment of long-term unemployed graduates: digital platform, priority >40 y, >10 y since graduation, one per family; file + "qualification" phase, batches over ≤3 years. **No written exam ⇒ not an exam-prep market.** Implementing decree not yet published (Sept 2026). [lapresse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/12/24/recrutement-des-diplomes-du-superieur-en-chomage-de-longue-duree-publication-de-la-loi-relative-au-jort-et-entree-en-vigueur-de-ses-dispositions/) · [webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/diplomes-chomeurs-neuf-mois-apres-la-loi-lunion-reclame-toujours-son-application/403473/)

## 4. Concours families
### Sûreté nationale (Intérieur)
- **Agents (حافظ أمن)** 2025: 1,950 posts over two concours; written test 13 Apr 2025. New round: registration 26 Nov–14 Dec 2025, written 15 Mar 2026. Male, 4ème année secondaire, 20–24 y. Phases: regional written (essay culture générale + QCM) → central: medical, sport, psychotechnique, oral, jury. [tunisienumerique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/la-tunisie-lance-une-campagne-de-recrutement-pour-1-950-agents-de-securite-en-2025/) · [kedma](https://www.kedma.tn/2025/11/police.html)
- **Officiers de police adjoints** (judiciaire): bac, M/F, 20–24, ≥1.70/1.65 m; registration 6–26 Jul 2026; written 4 Oct 2026. [tuniscope](https://www.tuniscope.com/ar/article/435477/actu-arabe/arabe/concours-ministere-interier-tunisie-484711)
- **Lieutenants principaux**: bac+5, 20–30, registration 10–29 Jul 2026. [lapresse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/10/min-de-linterieur-ouverture-dun-concours-pour-recruter-des-lieutenants-principaux-de-la-surete-nationale-et-de-la-police-nationale/)
- Candidates: **unknown**.
### Garde nationale
- 1,500 recruits planned 2026. Agents (عرفاء) border units: 4ème secondaire, 20–24, registration 5–24 Aug 2026, medical+written from 26 Oct 2026. [lapresse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/28/ministere-de-linterieur-ouverture-dun-concours-pour-le-recrutement-dagents-de-la-garde-nationale/)
- Sous-officiers techniques: 9ème année, 20–22, ≥1.70 m, vision ≥15/20; written (Arabic) 27 Jul 2026 then medical, physical, oral, psychological. [tuniscope](https://www.tuniscope.com/article/432827/actualites/tunisie/concours-garde-nationale-2026-inscriptions-ouvertes-des-maintenant-450611)
- Written exam (caporaux) in Arabic: culture générale essay + QCM. [interieur.gov.tn](https://www.interieur.gov.tn/actualite/30310/)
### Protection civile
- 280 caporaux (2025): 4ème secondaire, 20–24 (25 with military service), 1.70/1.65 m, vision 15/20; registration 8–19 Dec 2025; written 8 Feb 2026. 2023/24: 200. 2026 budget: 400. [lapresse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/12/06/protection-civile-le-ministere-de-linterieur-ouvre-un-concours-externe/)
### Douane
- 100 officiers (2025): licence/maîtrise, single, ≤27, 1.70/1.65 m; IT 8, law 14, eco/management 42, accounting/finance 30, PE 2, English 4. [douane.gov.tn PDF](https://www.douane.gov.tn/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/2025-08-07_concours_sous-lieutenant-2025.pdf)
- 250 sergents: arrêté 28 Nov 2025; deadline 31 Dec 2025; exams from 16 Feb 2026; via concours.douane.gov.tn. [webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-la-douane-lance-une-campagne-de-recrutement-denvergure/388785/)
- Lieutenants written test = QCM; results Jul 2026. [lapresse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/21/concours-de-la-douane-les-resultats-de-lepreuve-ecrite-publies/)
